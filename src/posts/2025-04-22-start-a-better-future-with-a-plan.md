A better financial future rarely comes from a single big decision. It comes from a plan you can actually follow — one built around your goals, your income, and the people who depend on you.

## Three questions to begin with

1. **What are you working toward?** Retirement, a home, your children's education, or simply the freedom to handle the unexpected.
2. **What could knock you off course?** An illness, a job loss, or an emergency expense — and how would your family cope today?
3. **What can you do consistently?** A plan you follow beats a perfect plan you abandon.

## Build the foundation first

Before investing for growth, make sure the basics are solid: an emergency fund, the right insurance coverage, and a clear picture of your monthly cash flow. This foundation is what lets your investments do their job without being cashed in at the wrong moment.

## Grow with purpose

Once the foundation is in place, a customized investment plan helps your money work toward the goals you named — with a level of risk that lets you sleep at night.
