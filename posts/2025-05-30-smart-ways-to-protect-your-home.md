Your home is likely your largest asset, and protecting it well is part of a sound financial plan. The good news: strong protection and reasonable premiums aren't mutually exclusive. A few smart moves can do both.

## Review your coverage, not just your price

The cheapest policy isn't a bargain if it leaves you exposed. Start by confirming your coverage actually reflects what it would cost to rebuild your home today — then look for savings within that.

## Bundle and simplify

Combining home and auto coverage with the same provider often lowers both premiums. Fewer policies to track also means fewer gaps and renewal surprises.

## Reduce risk to reduce cost

Updated wiring, a modern roof, security systems, and leak detectors all lower the odds of a claim — and insurers often reward that with lower rates. Protecting your home physically and protecting it financially go hand in hand.

## Revisit it yearly

Rates and needs both change. An annual check ensures you're never overpaying for yesterday's situation or underinsured for today's.
