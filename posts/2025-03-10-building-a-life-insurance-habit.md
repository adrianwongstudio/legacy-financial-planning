Insurance is one of those things many families mean to sort out "someday." But the peace of mind it provides only shows up when a policy is actually in place. Turning coverage into a habit — reviewed regularly and updated as life changes — is what separates a plan that protects your family from one that only looks good on paper.

## Start with what matters most

Before comparing policies, get clear on what you're protecting: your income, your mortgage, your children's education, and the lifestyle you want your family to keep if something happens to you. When the "why" is concrete, choosing the right coverage becomes far simpler.

## Review it at least once a year

Life changes — a new home, a new child, a raise, a business. Each of these can shift how much coverage you need. A quick annual review keeps your policy aligned with your real life instead of the life you had when you first signed up.

## Make it automatic

Set premiums to pay automatically and tie your policy review to a date you'll remember, like the start of the year or a birthday. Small, consistent habits protect your family far more reliably than big one-time decisions.
